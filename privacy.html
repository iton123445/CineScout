Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

CineScout is a free, non-commercial movie discovery site. We collect as little as possible, we don't sell your data, and we don't run ads or tracking analytics.

What we collect

If you just browse

  • Nothing that identifies you. We don't use analytics, advertising cookies or tracking pixels.
  • Your browser stores a few preferences on your own device (local storage): your streaming country, the name you last used for a guest comment, and whether you've already seen the intro this visit. You can clear these anytime in your browser settings.

If you comment as a guest

  • The name you type and your comment. Both are public and shown on that movie's page.

If you create an account

  • Email address: used to sign you in, confirm your account and reset your password. It is never shown publicly.
  • Password: stored only as a secure hash by our authentication provider. We can't see it.
  • Display name: shown publicly on your comments.
  • Your activity: My List (private to you), your star ratings (counted anonymously in each movie's average), and your comments (public).
  • A sign-in session token kept in your browser so you stay logged in.

How we use it

Only to run the features you use: signing in, saving your list, showing ratings and comments, and sending account emails (confirmation and password reset). We don't use your data for advertising, profiling or marketing, and we never sell or rent it.

Services that process data

CineScout runs on these third-party services. When your browser loads them, they may receive your IP address and standard browser information under their own privacy policies:

  • Supabase: account sign-in and database for lists, ratings and comments (privacy policy).
  • Render: hosts this website and may keep basic server logs (privacy policy).
  • TMDB: movie information, posters and streaming availability (privacy policy).
  • YouTube: trailers, embedded in privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com). YouTube may set cookies once a trailer plays (Google privacy policy).
  • Internet Archive: free public-domain films and their player (terms & privacy).
  • Google Fonts, jsDelivr and flagcdn: fonts, a code library and country flag images.

Links to streaming services such as Netflix or Prime Video take you to their websites, where their own privacy policies apply.

Your choices and rights

  • Change your display name or password: Account settings (click your initial in the top bar).
  • Delete a comment: use “Delete” on any comment you posted while signed in.
  • Delete your account: Account settings → Delete account. This permanently erases your account, list, ratings and comments right away.
  • Guest comments aren't linked to an account. To have one removed, report it or contact us (below).

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the Philippine Data Privacy Act), you may have rights to access, correct or erase your data. Contact us and we'll help.

Data retention and security

Account data is kept until you delete your account. Connections use HTTPS, and database rules ensure people can only change their own data. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a password you don't use elsewhere.

Children

CineScout isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, open an issue on our GitHub page. Please don't post personal details publicly there; just ask us to get in touch.
